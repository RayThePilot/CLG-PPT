## 1. Overview

**Name:** HomeBuddy
**Tagline:** Find your place. Find your people.
**One-line pitch:** HomeBuddy matches people who live away from home with compatible flatmates and verified rooms, PGs, and flats, with no broker.
**Category:** Housing + social matching marketplace (two-sided platform).

## 2. Problem

1. **Broker cost:** Newcomers often pay about one month's rent as brokerage.
2. **Flatmate risk:** Strangers clash over sleep time, cleanliness, food habits, guests, smoking, noise, and sharing bills.
3. **Fragmented search:** People look through Facebook groups, WhatsApp groups, and listing apps, and still can't tell who they would live with.
4. **Trust and safety:** Fake listings, unverified people, and no reviews. Women and people moving alone feel this most.
5. **Owner side:** PG owners and landlords have vacant beds, no reliable way to find tenants, and no tenant screening.
6. **Hidden costs:** Deposits, furniture, food, Wi-Fi, and moving are all handled separately.

## 3. Target Users

### Seekers (demand)
- School and college students (including those from other states)
- Interns and trainees
- First-job working professionals
- Job switchers relocating to a new city
- Competitive exam aspirants in coaching cities

### Suppliers (supply)
- PG owners and hostel operators
- Landlords with flats
- Existing tenants with a spare bed or room (flatmate needed)
- Service partners: tiffin and mess providers, furniture rental, movers, Wi-Fi providers

## 4. Solution and Features

### 4.1 Core features (must be in prototype)
1. **Sign up and onboarding:** Phone/email, role selection (seeker / owner).
2. **Lifestyle compatibility quiz:** Sleep time, wake time, cleanliness, cooking vs eating out, diet (veg / non-veg / egg), smoking, drinking, guests, noise, study or work hours, introvert/extrovert, pets, budget.
3. **Compatibility score:** A percentage match with each person, with a breakdown of why ("both night owls, both vegetarian, budget differs by 10%").
4. **Listings search:** Filter by city, area, budget, room type (private, shared, PG bed, flat), gender preference, amenities, distance to college or office.
5. **Listing details page:** Photos, rent, deposit, rules, amenities, owner verification badge, current occupants' compatibility with you.
6. **Flatmate discovery:** Browse profiles of people looking in the same area, sorted by compatibility.
7. **Verified badge:** Visual indicator for ID-verified users and owners.
8. **In-app chat:** Talk before sharing phone numbers (mock for prototype).
9. **Shortlist / save:** Save places and people.

### 4.2 Differentiating features
1. **Group formation (Squad Up):** Matched people form a group (for example three people) and apply together for a 2BHK or 3BHK, which lowers cost per person.
2. **Rent split calculator:** Fair split based on room size, attached bath, and so on.
3. **Trial week / meet-first flow:** Video call or meet at a public place before committing (suggested flow).
4. **Women-only and gender-specific modes:** Women-only listings and matching.
5. **Roommate agreement template:** A short digital house-rules sheet (chores, guests, quiet hours, bill split) both sides accept.
6. **Reviews and ratings:** Mutual ratings after a stay (cleanliness, respect, payment on time).

### 4.3 Add-on services (revenue and stickiness)
- Tiffin and mess subscription (connects to our earlier MessMate idea)
- Furniture and appliance rental
- Packers and movers
- Wi-Fi and utility setup
- Cleaning services
- Rent payment and bill splitting inside the app

### 4.4 Future features (roadmap, not for prototype)
- AI-based chat assistant for finding places
- Rental agreement e-signing and police verification help
- Roommate dispute mediation
- Credit-linked rent payments
- Student/employer partnerships (colleges, companies, coaching institutes)
- Expansion to co-living brands

## 5. User Journeys

### Seeker journey
1. Opens app, signs up, verifies ID.
2. Takes the lifestyle quiz (2 minutes).
3. Sets city, area, budget, room type.
4. Sees "Top matches" (people) and "Best places" (listings).
5. Chats with a match, forms a squad, or applies for a place.
6. Visits and confirms. Pays the small success fee.
7. Uses add-ons (tiffin, furniture). Leaves a review.

### Owner journey
1. Signs up as owner, completes verification.
2. Lists a property with photos, rent, rules.
3. Receives screened, compatible tenant requests.
4. Fills vacancy and pays a listing/success fee.
5. Gets rating and repeat tenants.

## 6. Competition

| Competitor | What they do | Gap HomeBuddy fills |
|---|---|---|
| NoBroker, Housing.com, MagicBricks | Property listings | No flatmate matching |
| Facebook / WhatsApp groups | Informal flatmate search | No verification, no compatibility, high scam risk |
| PG / co-living brands (Zolo, Stanza Living, and similar) | Own managed properties | Higher price, limited choice, they don't match individuals across properties |
| Flatmate-finding sites | Find a flatmate | Usually not integrated with listings and verification in India |

Verify the competitor list and their current features before the final PPT.

**Our edge:** Place and people matching in one app, plus verification, plus group renting, plus add-ons.

## 7. Business Model

| Revenue stream | Who pays | How |
|---|---|---|
| Vacancy listing fee | Owners / PGs | Per listing or monthly plan |
| Success fee | Owner or seeker | Small one-time fee when a rental closes (far below one month's rent) |
| HomeBuddy Plus | Seekers | Monthly plan: priority matching, unlimited chats, verified badge (estimate: ₹99-199) |
| Featured listings | Owners | Pay to rank higher |
| Partner commissions | Service partners | Percentage on tiffin, furniture, movers, Wi-Fi |
| Ads / partnerships (later) | Brands | Banners, sponsored offers |

### Illustrative unit economics (estimate, to be refined)
- Success fee: ₹500-1,000 per closed rental.
- Plus subscription: ₹149/month, with a small share of users subscribing.
- Add-on commission: 10-15% on partner services.

## 8. Go-to-Market

1. **Phase 1 (months 0-3):** One locality near several colleges and offices in one city. Onboard 30-50 PGs/landlords manually. Seed users via college groups and campus ambassadors.
2. **Phase 2 (months 3-9):** Cover the whole city, add add-on services, start the paid plan.
3. **Phase 3 (year 2):** Expand to other student/IT hub cities. Partner with colleges, companies, coaching centers.

**Marketing channels:** Campus ambassadors, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts about roommate horror stories, WhatsApp referrals (refer a friend, get a discount), partnerships with college hostels and HR departments, local SEO.

## 9. Costs and Funding (estimate)

**Initial costs:** App development, server and hosting, verification service fees, marketing and ambassadors, owner onboarding team, legal setup.
**Running costs:** Cloud hosting, ID verification API, customer support, marketing, payment gateway fees.
**Funding options:** Bootstrapping, college incubation cell, startup competitions, angel investors.

## 10. Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Safety / fraud | ID verification, owner verification, reports, reviews, chat before sharing numbers |
| Cold start (no users and no listings) | Launch in one locality, onboard owners manually first |
| Users bypass the platform to avoid fees | Offer value after matching: agreement, rent payments, add-ons, reviews |
| Compatibility score isn't trusted | Show reasons for each score, improve it with feedback |
| Privacy of personal data | Minimal data collection, consent, secure storage, clear policy |
| Legal compliance (tenancy, data protection) | Terms and conditions, legal advice, follow local rules |

## 11. Success Metrics (KPIs)
- Number of verified users and listings
- Matches formed and rentals closed
- Time to find a place (target: days, not weeks)
- Average match rating after move-in
- Repeat usage and referral rate
- Monthly revenue

## 12. Social Impact
- Cuts broker costs for people on tight budgets.
- Makes shared living safer, especially for women and people moving alone.
- Helps owners earn steady income from vacant beds.
- Reduces loneliness in a new city.

**Stack suggestion:** React + Vite, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion (animations), Lucide icons, React Router.

**Screens:**
1. Landing page (hero, how it works, features, testimonials)
2. Sign up / login (mock)
3. Lifestyle quiz (multi-step with progress bar)
4. Dashboard: top matches and best places
5. Listings search with filters
6. Listing detail page
7. Flatmate profile with compatibility breakdown
8. Squad Up (group formation)
9. Chat (mock)
10. Owner dashboard (add listing, view requests)

**Design direction:** Clean, premium, modern. Calm primary color (deep indigo or teal) with a warm accent (coral or amber), soft neutrals, rounded cards, subtle glass effects, smooth page transitions, hover micro-interactions, animated compatibility ring, skeleton loaders.
